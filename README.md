# Pitch it

> Built at **Startup Weekend Žilina** 🇸🇰

## The problem

Many founders have a great idea and then freeze up when they pitch it on stage. Their gestures, pacing and structure undercut what they're trying to say, and good feedback usually means finding a mentor and waiting for them.

## The solution

**Pitch it** is an AI pitch coach in your browser. You practice the pitch and it coaches your delivery live. When you finish, it gives you personalized feedback built on real knowledge of startup pitching and of what investors look for.

## How it works

1. **Personalized onboarding.** Open the web app on your PC and go through a short onboarding flow that learns who you are and what you're pitching.
2. **Set your goals.** Tell it exactly what you're after: how much money you're raising, who your audience is, how long the pitch is, and what you want to improve.
3. **Pitch live.** Talk to your webcam. A vision model tracks your body language in real time and shows you instantly how you're doing:
   - 🟢 / 🔴 **color auras** around you for good vs. weak gestures
   - 👐 🙌 😬 **emoji reactions** for open hands, closed posture, fidgeting and similar
4. **AI analysis.** When you finish, a loading indicator appears while the AI reviews the whole pitch. It uses RAG over a knowledge base of startup pitching, investor expectations and proven pitch structures.
5. **Personalized lessons.** You get feedback tailored to your goals and to your actual performance, as text and as **playable audio** so you can listen and practice again.

## Key features

- Real-time gesture and body-language feedback
- Goal-based, personalized coaching
- RAG-powered knowledge of pitching and investors
- Post-pitch report with concrete lessons
- Audio playback of feedback

## Tech (planned)

- **Frontend:** web app (desktop and webcam first)
- **Live gesture tracking:** open-source pose / movement model from Hugging Face
- **Feedback engine:** LLM + RAG over a startup-pitch knowledge base
- **Audio:** text-to-speech for spoken lessons
